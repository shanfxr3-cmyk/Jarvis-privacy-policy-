Jarvis – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Jarvis is a personal Android assistant app built and used by its developer (Shan). This page explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, Jarvis may access data from your own Google account, such as your YouTube channel and analytics, Gmail, Calendar, Drive, and Tasks. It only accesses the data covered by the permissions you approve on the Google consent screen.

How data is used

Data is used only to perform the actions you ask for inside the app, for example showing your channel stats, reading or drafting an email, or creating a calendar event. Text needed to answer your request may be sent to AI providers (such as Google Gemini or Groq) for processing.

Storage and sharing

Sign-in tokens and settings are stored locally on your device. Jarvis does not run its own servers, does not sell your data, and does not share it with third parties except the AI and API providers named above, solely to fulfil your request. No advertising is shown.

Google API Services disclosure

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

You can sign out in the app at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
